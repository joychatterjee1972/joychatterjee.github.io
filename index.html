The practice

Every face begins with observation.

Painted by hand.
Seen slowly.

For over two decades, Joy Chatterjee has worked with the human face as both subject and story — studying expression, character, light and the smallest details that make a person unmistakably themselves.

His portraits are created from observation and translated slowly onto canvas, using traditional painting techniques to pursue an almost photographic degree of realism.

The work has been presented to Kapil Dev, Sourav Ganguly and Shah Rukh Khan, and is held in the Boroline House collection and by private collectors in India and Italy.
