The Practice
Every face begins with observation.

For over two decades, Joy Chatterjee has dedicated his practice to hyper-realistic portraiture — studying expression, character and the smallest details that make a person unmistakably themselves.

Each portrait is painted by hand from a photograph, with the intention of creating something that feels less like an image and more like a presence.

Presented to Kapil Dev, Sourav Ganguly and Shah Rukh Khan. Held in the Boroline House collection and by private collectors in India and Italy.
