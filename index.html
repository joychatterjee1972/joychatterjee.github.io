About the artist

Every face begins with observation.

For over two decades, Joy Chatterjee has devoted his practice to the study of faces, expression and character through hyper-realistic portraiture.

His work has travelled from West Bengal across India and to Italy, combining traditional hand-painted technique with an obsessive attention to the smallest details of a human face.

Presented to Kapil Dev, Sourav Ganguly and Shah Rukh Khan. Held in the Boroline House collection and by private collectors in India and Italy.
