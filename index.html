For more than two decades, Joy Chatterjee has dedicated his practice to the art of portraiture.

Working by hand, each portrait is built through careful observation — studying light, expression, texture and the small details that give a face its identity.

His artistic journey has taken him from West Bengal across India and to Italy, developing a distinctive approach to hyper-realistic portraiture.
