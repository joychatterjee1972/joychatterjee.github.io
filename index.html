Every face begins with observation. The painting begins where the photograph ends.

Every face begins with observation.

For over two decades, I have painted faces by hand — studying expression, character, light and the smallest details that make a person unmistakably themselves.

My practice has developed across West Bengal — India — Italy, with a focus on hyper-realistic portraiture.

My portraits have been presented to Kapil Dev, Sourav Ganguly and Shah Rukh Khan. The work is held in the Boroline House collection and by private collectors in India and Italy.
