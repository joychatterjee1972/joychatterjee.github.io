The Practice
Twenty years of looking closely.

For over two decades, Joy Chatterjee has devoted his practice to hyper-realistic portraiture, studying expression, character and the details that make a person unmistakably themselves.

Working from photographs, he paints each portrait by hand, translating a captured moment into a lasting presence on canvas.

His portraits have been presented to Kapil Dev, Sourav Ganguly and Shah Rukh Khan, and are held in the Boroline House collection and by private collectors in India and Italy.
