The artist / 02

Every face
begins with
observation.

Over two decades of painting faces by hand. Every portrait begins with observation — capturing the expression, character and details that make a person unmistakably themselves.

A journey in hyper-realistic portraiture, from West Bengal to India and Italy.

Presented to Kapil Dev, Sourav Ganguly and Shah Rukh Khan. Held in the Boroline House collection and by private collectors in India and Italy.
