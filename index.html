How a commission works

Simple and personal, from the first message to the day your portrait arrives.

  1. Send a photograph

    Share the photo you’d like painted on WhatsApp or email. Joy will tell you honestly whether it has enough detail.

  2. Agree size and price

    Choose the size. You receive a written quote and timeline, and an advance reserves your place.

  3. Watch it take shape

    Progress photos as the portrait develops, with a chance to share thoughts before the final details.

  4. Receive your portrait

    Collected, delivered, or carefully packed and shipped, including overseas.

Ready when you are.

Questions

What kind of photograph do I need?

A clear, well-lit photo where the face is sharp works best. Send what you have; Joy will say honestly whether it has enough detail.

How much does a portrait cost?

It depends on the size, the number of people and the level of detail. You’ll receive a written quote after Joy has seen your photograph.

How long does it take?

Each portrait is painted entirely by hand, so time depends on size and detail. You’ll get a clear timeline with your quote.

Can I order from outside India?

Yes. Joy has painted commissions for clients in Italy. Shipping can be arranged with careful packing.
