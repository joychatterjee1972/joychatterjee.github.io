    The Practice

    Twenty years of looking closely.

    For over two decades, Joy Chatterjee has devoted his practice to hyper-realistic portraiture, studying expression, character and the details that make a person unmistakably themselves.

    Working from photographs, he paints each portrait by hand, translating a captured moment into a lasting presence on canvas.

    The Process

    From photograph to portrait.

    1. Send a photograph

      Share the photo you’d like painted on WhatsApp or email. Joy will tell you honestly whether it has enough detail.

    2. Discuss the portrait

      Agree the size together. You receive a written quote and timeline, and an advance reserves your place.

    3. Watch it take shape

      Progress photos as the portrait develops, with a chance to share thoughts before the final details.

    4. Receive the original

      Collected, delivered, or carefully packed and shipped, including overseas.
